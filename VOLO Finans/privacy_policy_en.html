Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the VOLO Finance mobile application ("App") handles your personal data. Your privacy is important to us, and the App has been designed with the principle of collecting as little data as possible.

VOLO Finance does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data with third parties.

1. Data Collection

The App does not collect any personal data. Specifically:

  • No identity information such as name, email address, or phone number is requested or collected.
  • No location data is collected.
  • No usage analytics or tracking is performed.
  • No advertising identifiers or device fingerprints are captured.
  • No account creation or sign-in is required.

2. Data Stored on Device

The following data is stored locally on your device only and is never transmitted outside the device under any circumstances:

  • Portfolio information (asset names, quantities, purchase prices, purchase dates)
  • Sell transaction records
  • Watchlist preferences
  • App settings (language, sort order, etc.)

This data is permanently deleted when the App is uninstalled or the device is reset. The App provides a backup feature that allows you to export your data in JSON format. The storage and security of the exported file is entirely your responsibility.

3. Third-Party Services

The App connects to third-party API services to display current market data. During these connections:

  • Only the requested asset symbol (e.g., "TUPRS") is sent to the API provider.
  • Your personal information, portfolio data, or transaction history is never sent to third-party services.
  • Third-party API providers are subject to their own privacy policies, over which the developer has no control.

4. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Since the App does not collect any personal data whatsoever, this risk does not exist.

5. Data Security

Since all your data is stored locally on your device, data security is directly dependent on your device's security measures (screen lock, encryption, etc.). Ensuring the security of your device is your responsibility.

6. Your Rights

Since the App does not collect any personal data, requests for data access, correction, or deletion do not apply. You can delete locally stored data at any time by uninstalling the App.

7. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes take effect as soon as they are published within the App. In the event of significant changes, a notification will be provided within the App.
