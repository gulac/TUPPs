Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Seventh Sense processes, why it is needed, who receives it, how long it is kept, and the rights and choices available to you.

Effective date: September 26, 2026 · Version: 1.3

Depending on the feature, we send the AI provider a name or nickname, relationship context, chart-derived indicators, and questions you write. 1. Who we are

Seventh Sense is developed and operated by Uzeyir Gulac, an individual developer in Ankara, Türkiye, under the name GLC Studios (“GLC Studios,” “we,” “us,” or “our”). Uzeyir Gulac is the data controller responsible for personal information processed through Seventh Sense.

Email: seventhsense@glcstudios.com

This policy applies to the Seventh Sense mobile application, its widgets and notifications, these web pages, and the backend services used to provide them (together, “the Service”).

2. Information we process

Information you provide

  • Names or nicknames for you and for people you add.
  • Birth date, birth time (if known), and birthplace, plus the coordinates and time zone derived from the birthplace.
  • Relationship type, communication context, and preferences you choose.
  • Questions and situations you write into AI-powered features.
  • Notification preferences and your preferred notification time.
  • Anything you choose to include when you email support.

Information created by the Service

  • Natal chart, transit, synastry, house, aspect, and persona-chart calculations.
  • AI-generated or rule-based interpretations of those calculations.
  • An anonymous account identifier. We do not ask for your email address, phone number, or password.
  • Usage counters, budget and quota records, cache records, and security records.
  • If you enable notifications: a push notification token, your time zone, limited natal chart positions used to personalize the daily message, and delivery status.
  • App and device integrity signals from Apple App Attest/DeviceCheck and Firebase App Check.
  • Technical logs such as request time, requested endpoint, response status, and error type. Our application logs are designed not to contain full birth details or the text of your questions. To count how many different users use a feature on a given day, logs contain a pseudonymous identifier that changes every day and cannot be turned back into your account identifier. Infrastructure providers may create their own operational logs under their applicable terms.
  • Crash reports: if the app crashes or freezes, a report with the technical crash details (stack trace), app version, device model, operating system version, and a random installation identifier. Crash reports do not include your names, birth details, relationship context, questions, or interpretations.
  • Usage analytics, only if you opt in: in-app events such as opening a screen or using a feature (with fixed values such as a relationship type, never free text), session and engagement information, a random app-instance identifier, device model, operating system and app version, and country or region derived from your connection. Your names, birth details, questions, and interpretations are never included, and no advertising identifier is collected.

Information that stays on your device

Profile photos are stored only on your device. They are never sent to our backend or to the AI provider. Local copies of profiles, charts, preferences, and generated insights are also stored on your device so the app opens quickly and works reliably. Data stored on your device may be included in your own iCloud or device backups, depending on your Apple settings. Those backups are controlled by you and Apple.

Birthplace search

When you search for a birthplace, the text you type is sent to Apple’s location search service (Apple Maps) to find matching places. Apple handles this under its own privacy policy. We do not access your device’s location.

3. Purposes and legal bases

Where the law requires a legal basis (for example, the EU/UK GDPR or Türkiye’s Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698, “KVKK”), we rely on the following:

  • Providing the Service you request: chart calculations, widgets, notifications you enable, and in-app data deletion. Legal basis: performance of our contract with you (the Terms of Use).
  • AI-generated insights and answers: sending the limited information described in Section 4 to our AI provider. Legal basis: your consent, given by actively enabling the optional AI feature. You can withdraw it at any time.
  • Security, abuse prevention, and cost control: anonymous authentication, integrity checks, quotas, budgets, and fraud prevention. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in keeping the Service secure, fair, and financially sustainable.
  • Diagnosing failures and keeping the Service reliable: minimal technical logs and crash reports without personal content. Legal basis: legitimate interest.
  • Understanding how features are used, to improve the app: optional usage analytics described in Section 4. Legal basis: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • Responding to you: support and privacy requests. Legal basis: legitimate interest, or legal obligation where a law requires us to respond.
  • Legal compliance: keeping or disclosing information when the law requires it. Legal basis: legal obligation.

We do not use your information to train AI models, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling unrelated to the features you use.

4. AI processing and your consent

AI-generated insights are off by default. Information is sent to the AI provider only after you actively enable the optional AI feature. Accepting the Terms of Use alone does not turn AI features on.

When AI features are enabled, our backend sends only what each request needs to OpenAI:

  • the name or nickname entered for the relevant profile,
  • relationship type and context,
  • astrological indicators derived from calculated charts (for example, planet signs and aspects), and
  • the text of any question you write.

Birth dates, birth times, birthplaces, coordinates, photos, and your account identifier are not sent to OpenAI. Planetary positions are calculated by our own backend; the AI only explains those results.

Under OpenAI’s API data policies, information sent through the API is not used to train OpenAI’s models. OpenAI may keep it for a limited time (currently up to 30 days) to monitor for abuse, and then deletes it unless the law requires longer retention.

You can withdraw consent at any time by turning off AI insights in the You tab. Withdrawal stops future AI processing. It does not affect processing that already happened. Chart calculations and rule-based content keep working without AI.

Optional usage analytics

After setup, the app asks once whether you want to share usage data. Both choices are equally available, and nothing is sent unless you choose to share. If you agree, the usage information described in Section 2 is processed by Google Firebase Analytics to show us which features are used and how often. Advertising features, Google signals, data sharing with Google, and granular location and device collection are turned off, and the data is not used for advertising.

You can change your choice at any time with Share Usage Data in the You tab. Turning it off stops collection and resets the analytics identifier on your device. Refusing or withdrawing does not limit any feature of the app.

Crash reports

To find and fix errors, the app sends crash reports through Google Firebase Crashlytics. They contain only the technical information described in Section 2 and are collected regardless of your analytics choice, because they are needed to keep the app working.

9. Retention

We keep information only as long as it is needed for the purposes above. Server records expire automatically:

  • Daily insight and widget caches: about 7 days (15 minutes for fallback content).
  • Daily quota and daily budget records: about 45 days.
  • Pending AI budget reservations: about 2 days.
  • Reusable personalized-response caches (generated text, which may include a name or nickname and relationship context) and monthly budget records: up to 100 days. This avoids repeating AI processing and helps control abuse.
  • Notification registrations: while notifications are enabled. They are removed when you turn notifications or AI insights off, use in-app deletion, or when Apple reports that the notification token is no longer valid.
  • Device-status security records: about 180 days.
  • Technical logs, including the daily pseudonymous usage records: typically 30 days.
  • Crash reports: 90 days in Firebase Crashlytics.
  • Usage analytics (if you opted in): event- and user-level data for 2 months in Google Analytics. Aggregated reports that cannot identify you may be kept longer.
  • Support emails: as long as needed to resolve your request, and generally no longer than 2 years.

Local data stays on your device until you delete it, use in-app deletion, or remove the app.

If you remove the app without using in-app deletion, you may lose the ability to access the anonymous account and request deletion of records linked to it. Some records expire under the periods above; notification records may remain until the notification token is invalidated or otherwise removed. Use Permanently delete all data before removing the app if you want the app to initiate deletion.

10. Deleting your data

Open the You tab and choose Permanently delete all data. This deletes your anonymous account, all server records linked to it (caches, quotas, budgets, notification registrations, and device status records), and local profiles, insights, widget content, and settings on the device. It also resets the analytics identifier and your analytics choice on the device.

These limits apply:

  • Apple DeviceCheck provides two device-linked security bits and a timestamp that the app can query using a short-lived token. This version reads their status but does not write or change either bit. Any existing DeviceCheck state is managed by Apple and is not removed by in-app deletion.
  • Information already processed by OpenAI is subject to OpenAI’s limited retention described in Section 4. Backups and logs expire on their normal schedule.
  • Crash reports and any usage analytics already sent are not linked to your anonymous account, so we cannot single them out for deletion. They expire after the periods in Section 9.

Apple'ın yönettiği mevcut DeviceCheck durumu, uygulama verileri silindikten sonra kalabilir.

Uygulamayı bu kontrolü kullanmadan kaldırırsanız anonim hesaba erişimi ve onunla bağlantılı kayıtlar için silme işlemi başlatma imkânını kaybedebilirsiniz.
