Frequently asked questions

Why is an insight still loading?

The first chart interpretation can take longer, because several sections are prepared and cached. Keep the app open while it finishes. If daily content fails to load, check your connection and use Try Again on the home screen.

What does the AI see, and can I turn it off?

AI insights are off until you actively enable them in the app. When they are on, the name or nickname entered for the relevant profile, relationship context, chart-derived indicators, and questions you write may be sent to our AI provider. Birth dates, birth times, birthplaces, coordinates, photos, and the anonymous account identifier are not sent to that provider. You can turn AI off at any time in the You tab; chart calculations keep working. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Why did my content change after I changed the device language?

Generated interpretations are stored separately for each language. When the system language changes to a supported language, the app asks before it refreshes content, so nothing is regenerated unexpectedly.

Why does an unknown birth time matter?

Without a reliable birth time, the Ascendant, houses, and angles, and sometimes the Moon, cannot be stated precisely. Seventh Sense limits those claims instead of presenting uncertain information as exact.

My widget is not updating.

Try these steps:

  1. Open the app once after installing or updating it.
  2. Check that your main profile has a completed chart.
  3. Check that AI insights are enabled and your device has an internet connection.

iOS decides exactly when widgets refresh, so updates may not happen right at midnight.

I did not receive a notification.

In the You tab, check that daily notifications are turned on and check the selected time. Then open iOS Settings → Notifications → Seventh Sense. Focus modes and iOS delivery scheduling can also delay notifications.

How do I delete my account and data?

Open the You tab, scroll to Your data, and choose Permanently delete all data. After you confirm, the app deletes your anonymous account, the related server records, local profiles and insights, widget content, and notification settings. This cannot be undone.

Please do this before removing the app. Otherwise, you may lose access to the anonymous account and the ability to initiate deletion of records linked to it. Retention limits and exceptions are explained in the Privacy Policy.

Can I move my data to a new device?

Not at the moment. Seventh Sense uses an anonymous account without an email or password, so there is no account recovery or transfer yet.

Someone added my information. What can I do?

Profiles are stored on the device of the person who created them, and we cannot identify that device or profile from your name alone. Ask the person who added you to remove the profile and, where appropriate, use the in-app deletion control. You may contact us for an explanation of our data practices, but we may be unable to locate or alter an anonymous record that cannot be securely verified. See the Privacy Policy.

Is Seventh Sense professional advice?

No. The app is for entertainment, self-reflection, and communication inspiration. It does not provide medical, mental-health, legal, financial, or safety advice, and it should not be used for consequential decisions.

In crisis or feeling unsafe? Contact your local emergency number or a qualified support service right away. Seventh Sense cannot help in an emergency.
