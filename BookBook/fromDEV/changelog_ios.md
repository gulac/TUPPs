## 1.4

- You can now log reading for past days as well as today, from the page update screen.
- Added Demo mode to the Profile tab, so you can explore the app’s features with sample data.
- Improved the filters in the Library tab and added many new ones.
- Optimized the app for iPhone Duo.
- You can now record how many copies of a book you own.
- You can now note where a book is kept, such as at home, at the office or at a summer house, and choose its bookcase and shelf.
- You can now note where you finished a book and see these places on a map in the Stats tab.
- Reading streaks can now be weekly. You can choose daily or weekly in the Profile tab.
- Redesigned the Last activity screen with filters and search.

## 1.3

- Added sync across Apple devices. Your data stays up to date automatically on devices using the same Apple Account. You can turn on this Premium feature on each device in the Profile tab.
- You can now track books you received or gave as gifts, and books you borrowed or lent.
- Added shareable summary cards to Stats.
- You can now customize your library. Choose what to display in Profile > Library appearance.
- When you update your page from Apple Watch, the reading summary now also appears on your watch if that setting is on.
- Improved online book search. Books already in your library are now marked in the results.
- Adjusted Apple Watch colors for easier reading.
- Added progress bars to series and lists.
- You can now find your books in Spotlight. With new app shortcuts, you can ask Siri for your current page, update your page and start a reading session.
- Fixed an issue that showed the wrong days in the stats calendar.
- Improved translations and added Danish, Swedish, Polish and Norwegian.

## 1.2

- Your books are now on your wrist! Added Apple Watch support, so you can update your page and start, pause and finish reading sessions.
- You can now follow your book progress right on your watch face with Apple Watch widgets.
- Improved statistics. You can now review all the months of the year, past periods and detailed reading sessions.
- Expanded book language options. Different spellings of the same language now appear as a single option.
- Improved the daily goal view. When you beat your goal, your actual progress percentage is now shown.
- You can now view your reading rhythm by calendar week or the last 7 days.
- Book ratings now appear directly when you sort by rating.
- Added sorting by purchase date.
- Fixed page ranges in reading sessions and Last activity records.
- Made various improvements to the iPad interface and the app’s overall look.

## 1.1

- You can now add notes by scanning them with your camera.
- Added reading sessions and a timer.
- You can now choose the reading start date when you start a book.
- You can now mark the books you add as e-books.
- You can now add descriptions to iCloud backups, delete them and lock them.
- You can now edit book genres and add new ones from the Profile tab.
- The Completed books section now shows reading start and finish dates.
