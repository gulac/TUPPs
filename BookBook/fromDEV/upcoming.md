## In the works

Here is what we are building for the next versions of BookBook.

- **Weekly reading streak:** keep your streak by reading a set number of days each week.
- **Reading map:** see *where* you finished your books.
- **Copies and location:** note how many copies you own and where each one is kept.
- **Past-day reading logs:** add the pages you forgot to log.
